## SMS Terms

**Program name:** Photos by Yaz Session Alerts

**What you'll receive:** When you join the line for a Photos by Yaz photo session, we text you about your place in line. That includes a confirmation that you joined, an "up next" alert, a "your turn" alert, and occasional updates if your spot changes. We do not send marketing or promotional messages through this program.

**How you sign up:** You opt in by entering your mobile number and checking the text-message consent box on the session sign-up page, or by giving your number to Photos by Yaz for a scheduled session.

**Message frequency:** Varies by session, usually 2–5 messages per session.

**Cost:** Message and data rates may apply.

**Opt out:** Reply **STOP** at any time to stop receiving messages. You will receive one confirmation text and no further messages. Reply **START** to opt back in.

**Help:** Reply **HELP** for help, or contact us at [email] / [phone].

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

## Privacy Policy for Session Alerts

**What we collect:** Your name or party name, party size, the names of party members (optional), and your mobile phone number.

**How we use it:** Only to manage your place in line and send you session alerts. Other guests see your first name and last initial (for example, "Emma R.") on the live queue page. They never see your phone number.

**Sharing:** We do not sell, rent, or share your mobile number or personal information with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. We use a messaging provider (Twilio) only to deliver the texts described above.

**Retention:** Session data, including names and phone numbers, is automatically deleted **7 days after the session ends**, or sooner on request. If you reply STOP, we keep a scrambled (one-way hashed) record of your number so we can honor your opt-out in future sessions. The hashed record cannot be turned back into your number.
